It’s really kinda funny how the market for computer hardware is being warped into insanity by a mix of supply chain issues and increasingly strange workload markets.
🔗
Life used to be so simple. Consumers bought client devices, enterprises bought servers, and boring industries bought embedded parts. Gaming PCs were a cute side market.
🔗
Now, random crypto rig operations in garages, all sorts of crazy ML builds, strange new form factors on the edge. All kinds of IoT applications besides automotive. It’s a silicon wonderland.
🔗
Every week now I hear of people using computer hardware in ways that strike me as a new market.
🔗
The last mile of computing is going to be quite insane soon.

Desktops —> Laptops —> mobile —> Cambrian explosion of random insanity.

The home as a menagerie of a hundred weird computers. Not smart home so much as weirded home.
