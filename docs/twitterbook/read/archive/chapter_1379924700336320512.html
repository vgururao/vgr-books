In the last few years essay writing has gone really barbell. Things are either transparently politically motivated internet-of-beefs hit jobs, or things that leave me genuinely puzzled about why it was worth the effort at all. Stuff that reads like dada non sequiturs.
🔗
Stuff is fun to write when you only figure out why you wrote it after you’re done. Knowing before you start and never knowing at all both correlate to not having fun writing. If others are like me, most people are not having fun writing. They’re grinding. With it without an axe.
🔗
There just might be a glut of writing because there’s now distribution room to fill. Reminds me of when cable channels proliferated in the late 80s leading to a glut of content barely above lorem ipsum for a decade before quality content production started catching up.
🔗
Infrastructure expansion shock. Like cable glut caused cable industry collapse before streaming video eventually filled all the dark fiber a decade later. We’re building a glut of text pipes now. Increasingly confident the text Renaissance has turned into a text bubble.
