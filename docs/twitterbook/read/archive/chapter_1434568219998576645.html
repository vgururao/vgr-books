When/how/why does marshaling of useful potential turn into hoarding?

Examples I’m thinking of: charged battery, dam, stocked pantry, survivalist/emergency supplies, full factory inventory, “full-stack” engineer, decathlete, polymathery, polyglottery, multiple masters degrees…
🔗
In principle “potential” is eternal, abstract and undifferentiated, but in practice it is always transient, embodied and differentiated. One sign it is hoarding is increase in entropy without actualization of potential. Eg expired items.
🔗
Another sign is organizational disorder. Organization is easiest when you have some sense of general purpose I’d not specific goals. The less clear the purpose, the harder it is to organize the potential. In the limit of true purposelessness you can’t organize at all.
🔗
I recall in Oscar and Lucinda, the boy Oscar collects stones as a hobby and tries various schemes like by color or size. If there’s no purpose, there’s no good reason to choose one scheme over another. For many collection hobbies this is a source of pleasure.
🔗
Alternately, absent a telos, the most natural scheme is one that efficiently embodies the history of the potential. Kinda begs the question since your sense of history is a dual of your sense of future potential (eg “dead end” vs “frontier”). You grok both or neither 🤔
🔗
Darwinist org schemes are max potential/minimum telos ways of organizing resources 🤔
🔗
Related: the kinetic equivalent of potential. Some generalization of momentum. A flow rate times a “mass.”

Free cash flow. Calories/day. Stable demand. They’re a kind of potential. Predictably renewable flow.
