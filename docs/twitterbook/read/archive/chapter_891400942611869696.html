1/ Screw it, it's a tweetstormy Saturday. Let's do another one: do you get wiser with age? Preview of answer: not necessarily, but maybe.
🔗
2/ What is perceived as "wisdom" is actually an incredibly shallow behavior: production of pithy, aphoristic insight from a high-EQ persona
🔗
3/ The easy part first: you get better at reading emotional context with age, have more anecodata appropriate for different contexts.
🔗
4/ You have also found good ways to tell your stories through trial and error. These are not signs of wisdom but narrative polishing.
🔗
5/ About 99% of appearing sage is merely high-EQ appropriate affect and taming of knee-jerk responses. It's acting better, mellowing out.
🔗
6/ The evidence of your life and the scarred-over screwed-upness suggests that any sageness in affect is not inner light shining through
🔗
7/ Which leaves us with pithy aphorisms as main symptom of supposed wisdom. Pithiness has to do with insight density, aphorisms with length.
🔗
8/ Length is easier. You go from long-winded to aphoristic length in things you say largely due to falling cognitive endurance.
🔗
9/ Pithiness, or insight density: this does NOT change much over life. Any change is due to rare, sharp staircase-like jumps in awareness
🔗
10/ Some people go through life never leveling-up this way. Their insight compression ratio is about the same at 22 as at 42.
🔗
11/ Insight compression ratio is how much raw experience/learning (the boring story told with low self-awareness) can be compressed
🔗
12/ A boring tale of a boring vacation told with the obligatory accompanying boring photos has compression ratio ~3 or 4 maybe
🔗
13/ OTOH, a truly thoughtful person with a few staircase jumps under belt might tell the story of a complex project with 10:1 compression
🔗
14/ So appearance of wisdom is mostly high-EQ acting accompanied by unchanged pithiness talking that is aphoristic because of low endurance
🔗
15/ I've had maybe 2-3 staircase-jumps in my insight-density over 20y. About 90% of my compression ratio increases happened over ~10 days
🔗
16/ These days I tweet more, post short things on Facebook more. Longer blog posts are rarer, book ideas seem like impossible mountains.
🔗
17/ But I have NOT gotten pithier. My tweets have about the same compression ratio as my 4000 word blog posts. Last staircase jump was ~2006
🔗
18/ So be skeptical of appearance of wisdom. It's 99% an act masking shrinking horizons, falling cognitive endurance, static insightfulness
🔗
19/ One of the unspoken secret covenants among people of roughly the same age is to not call bullshit on each other's wisdom posturing
🔗
20/ It's like prisoner's dilemma cooperation against younger people. Cooperating cohorts seem to be ~5y long (25-30, 30-35, 35-40, 40-45...)
