What you might call Roslingism (after Hans Rosling, RIP) is a 3-step fallacious reasoning pattern:

1. Discard first-principles sociology as junk
2. Paint a pure economics/numbers based whitish picture
3. Do an invalid extrapolation to rosy alt-sociological conclusions
