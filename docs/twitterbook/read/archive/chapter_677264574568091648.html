I went to school in not-Boston. I did my few years of tech work in not-Silicon Valley. I've done/do all my writing in not-New-York.
🔗
Conjecture: If originality and independence are your primary considerations, not-there is probably the best place to do your work.
🔗
In generally the cost of originality and independence is that you must sacrifice almost every possible source of leverage
🔗
But if you can identify a clear source of leverage that would make a huge difference to your preferred form of creative work, just go there
🔗
There aren't many true kinds of leverage for my skills, so I didn't actually sacrifice much. If that's untrue for you, avoid not-there.
