The only hardware work I do regularly is cooking. Hardware generally has a higher, more complex Minimal Satisfying Project (MSP) threshold. In software, loosely defined to include writing, satisfaction can be found at the scale of making a good sentence, or a good Excel graph.
🔗
Though I suppose paper airplanes or other common origami designs are decent MSPs.

MSPs are a more useful idea for me than MVPs, since I rarely need anything I do to be viable. Only personally satisfying.
🔗
MSPs:

sentence
line graph
2x2
paper airplane
hot sandwich
replacing car wiper blades
assembling ikea furniture
🔗
though in general I don’t find pure “toy” MSPs very satisfying, it doesn’t have to be “useful” but it should be woven into regular life rather than a side hobby life. So a sandwich is more satisfying than a paper plane
