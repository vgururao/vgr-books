Q: What’s the difference between ontology and taxonomy

A: Ontology is the study of how to kill jokes about taxonomy
🔗
All my favorite jokes are ontological jokes. Let me list a few of my favorites. See if you can kill them all by explaining why they work.
🔗
Zapp Brannigan is a category of ontological jokes all by himself
🔗
A bit 101, but hilarious:

Zapp: If we can hit that bullseye the rest of the dominoes will fall like a house of cards: checkmate

“They’re both dictators, except for Amitabh Bachchan”
