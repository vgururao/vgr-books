Have you ever set/pursued real life goals? Not joke ones or fakes you make up for a workshop exercise.

I haven’t. I’ve only:

a) picked among opportunities (50%)

b) stirred the pot to create new opportunities (30%)

c) allowed random play to snowball into bigger thing (20%)
🔗
And not script-template goals like “find a romantic partner” or “finish college” that basically everybody pursues. Real ones that, if pursued either successfully or not, would be your unique story.
🔗
I’m very suspicious of people who claim to be pursuing goals. Elon Musk is a rare example of someone who auditably declares and pursues goals in public.
🔗
