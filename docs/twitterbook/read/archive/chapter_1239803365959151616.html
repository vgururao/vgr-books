Hmm, strikes me that I replayed The Room 1-3 series because now after several years because it has an escape room theme within various mansion-scale domestic cozy building settings. Very much recent headspace and now live condition. Room 4: Old Sins is set in a nice toy mansion.
🔗
Because of domestic cozy blogchain I’ve been thinking about home spaces for a year now. They’re under-theorized and oversentimentalized. The Room series really gets at the “hard cozy” or “dark cozy” aspect as in cozy murder mysteries, haunted houses, lovecraftian monsters etc.
🔗
We’re all gonna get to know our homes a lot more intimately than we’ve ever known them. Beyond their capacity for comfort and refuge to their innate mystery, hidden horrors, intellectual challenge, etc.
🔗
Also I’m realizing I’m a black-belt grade homebody. I haven’t left the building in 5 days and the apartment for 3 of those, and I’m totally fine with it. Not getting cabin fever. Wife otoh is getting really restless. I think I’d do fine for fairly long periods.
