My 80-year-old mom apparently spent part of the pandemic getting back into drawing for the first time since the 50s. Apparently drawing was one of her subjects through high school. I had no idea.
🔗
She’s surprisingly good… she’s been drawing from random photos, a few from life, and some exercises from a book etc. I left behind a book and sketch pad last trip randomly and she’s half filled it.
🔗
She’s also invented a new improv art… painting the leftover terracotta lamps from Diwali/Kartik and decorating them with glitter and stuff scavenged from rakhis (this is less surprising to me since she used to dabble in this kind of thing when I was a kid)
🔗
Funny how the pandemic revealed random new sides of people you thought you knew
