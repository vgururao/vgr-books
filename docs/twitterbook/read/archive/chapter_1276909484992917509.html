“Once you decide on an action, try giving up the goal and materially unnecessary …”
🔗
Once you decide on an action, try giving up the goal and materially unnecessary assumptions and check if action gut-feels valuable by itself.

Example:

Action: carry umbrella
Goal: stay dry
Unnecessary assumption: it is raining/will rain

Monkey say: Still fun to carry
🔗
Ideal: all actions should be functionally unfixed like this to the extent possible
🔗
Tired: be somebody or do something?
Wired: be doing
🔗
Dunno why people complicate these things. I don’t think either Laozi or Heidegger used umbrella grade examples in talking about the Tao or being-and-time.
🔗
Me 13 years ago, a qualified consequentialist: “The ends justify the means only if they [the ends] are first justifiable by themselves.”

Me now, a weirdified deontologist: The ends justify the means only if the means are first justifiable by themselves.
