Parkinson’s law: work expands to fill the time available

My law: Work shrinks to fit the gap between the procrastination and the deadline
🔗
I generally like to fulfill commitments to others on time but also like to procrastinate so I usually lower scope to fit the time. So I’m rarely ambitious if there’s a true hard deadline.

If I have soft deadlines or the commitment is to myself, both laws kick in.
🔗
Work squishes and expands as the procrastination chases the moving deadline
🔗
Amazed at people who are energized by deadlines.
