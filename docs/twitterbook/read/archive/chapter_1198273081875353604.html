Came up with a model I call the need/want/ignore social psychology model.

The world has 3 kinds of people in relation to you: people you need in your life, people you want in your life, and people you’re indifferent to. It’s not symmetric so there’s 9 kinds of relationship.
🔗
Everybody has a crazy side and a sane side. Your crazy side hooks up with the complementary crazy side of a few others to form the core co-dependencies you need in your life.

Need-need is private life.
🔗
Want-want is your sane side hooking up with the same sides of others creating your public life.

Ignore-ignore is your live and let live life.

These 3 are the main diagonal.
🔗
Off-diagonal pairs:

I-need-you-want: exploiter/exploiter

I-need-you-ignore and I-want-you-ignore is various patterns of dehumanization ranging from stalking to ghosting. Details left as homework.
🔗
Much less overlap than you’d think, so to first approximation most relationships are nearly pure examples of 1 of the 9.
🔗
Fill each cell with the number of people you have in each cell of the 3x3 and you have a picture of your entanglement in society. Computing the eigenvalues would probably reveal something.
🔗
You can see the role a particular social graph plays in your life by construct a matrix just from that and subtracting it from your full matrix.
🔗
