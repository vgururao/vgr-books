2/ In 2006, the Galactic Cryptospatial Navigators Guild was at war with the Great Central Singularity, I had escaped to Earth to avoid it
🔗

🔗
4/ Unfortunately thanks to the war, the hypertemporal futures markets based on the will-have-been-perhaps-traded commodities collapsed.
🔗
5/ The plans were worthless. Fortunately, I had a backup survival plan. There was great demand for Earth vitamins in parts of the Orion arm.
🔗
6/ So I spent a few months selling vitamins via an open low-fee hypershipping consignment channel run by a friend.
🔗
7/ Unfortunately, that didn't get me enough to book passage off Earth at galactic wartime rates, but it was enough to go native here
🔗
8/ I got myself some earthican surgery, a fake bio, and began adapting and porting some of the Refactorat plans for the Earth economy
🔗
9/ That got the blog rolling. The porting took some work. The Gervais Principle, for example, is based on sewage system of the Refactorat.
🔗
