Order of increasing consulting difficulty, w increasing likelihood of producing bs: senior execs < startup teams/individuals < middle mgrs

Consulting support for middle managers is the hardest kind. Failing and producing bullshit instead = definition of mainstream consulting.
🔗
Anything defined in terms of "deliverables" is for middle managers, even if presented to C-level, and is 90% likely to be bullshit.
🔗

🔗
