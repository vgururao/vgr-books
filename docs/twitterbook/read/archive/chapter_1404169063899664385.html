Poll: If you took a 10k business risk today, what would be the minimum return multiple in 5 years to make it interesting for you?

Assume modest, near-passive maintenance work not much higher than managing a stock purchase at that level
🔗
For those who think better in CAGR, 5x in 5y is a CAGR of 38%
🔗
2x is 14% CAGR, 10x is 58%.

These are value numbers that say nothing about liquidity. 10k may turn into 100k, but in the form of 30k realized dividend and 70k NPV-DCF for eg
🔗
For benchmarking the S&P has had a 13.67 CAGR over the last 10 years

Or about 2x every 5y for the last decade. I personally think business risks should model out to at least S&P under conservative assumptions.
