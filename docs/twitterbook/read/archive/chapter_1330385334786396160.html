In a way weather is a good analogy. Governance systems are like natural phenomena. Perhaps some are just more dangerous than others. Maybe living in a western liberal democracy is like living in an earthquake or hurricane zone. You get some nice views but you pay with risk.

I’m wary of all monocausal simple answers like prompt government action, SARS/MERS experience, etc. Backtrack a couple of degrees from proximal causes and those simple answers turn into politics-complete (as in NP-complete) questions.
🔗
Don’t want to let Western leaders off the hook, especially not Trump or BoJo, but it really is a full civilization stack A/B test here. It’s not path-dependent.
🔗
It’s easy to see with hindsight what tactics worked. It’s not at all clear why different sets of tactical options were available in different countries. And it’s not clear what price was paid over preceding decades to make those options available.
