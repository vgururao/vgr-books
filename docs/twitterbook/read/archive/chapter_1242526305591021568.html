Linear time:

Short term: 1 week - 1 quarter
Medium term: 1 quarter - 1 year
Long term: 1 year - 5 years
Eternity: > 5 years

Exponential time:

Short term: 1 week
Medium term: 2 weeks
Long term: 3 weeks
Eternity: > 4 weeks

🔗
