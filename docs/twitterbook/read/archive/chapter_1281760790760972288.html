I abuse language a lot in my writing both for lulz and specific rhetorical effects, but I do it pretty deliberately to match intent to abuse, and try not to debase language itself. Trumpist language abuse is also deliberate but is usually intended to debase language itself.

🔗
Debasing language is like randomly editing code and expecting it to run. Language is very much like software code, especially in the functioning of delicate but high-performance institutions. I really appreciate quality institutional communications. Or even just aiming at quality
🔗
You can’t trust an institution that lacks basic competence (and interest in competence) in its own boilerplate.
