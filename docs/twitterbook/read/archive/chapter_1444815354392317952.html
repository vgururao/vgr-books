“When you’ve written enough and people quote enough random bits back at you that …”
🔗
When you’ve written enough and people quote enough random bits back at you that you barely recognize, frequently enough to form a stream, it’s a kind of body horror mirror. Like a Dorian Gray painting of yourself. The quoteback stream gestalt is basically your inner monster.
🔗
While I have minor cringe responses to older writing, it doesn’t really bother me, and I don’t feel any need to delete or edit. But the quoteback stream… yeah. And the prob,em is worse if you’re primarily an essayist, since books control their own reaction exhaust better.
🔗
If you don’t want this kind of body horror, try to do all your writing as big fat books released every few years.
🔗
Newsletters worsen the effect in a way since they make it way harder to do continuous integration
