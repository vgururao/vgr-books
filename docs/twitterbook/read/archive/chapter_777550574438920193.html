Hmm aliasing is not really an abstraction leak 🤔
🔗
Abstraction leaks are interesting because they are the only way you can operate above highest abstraction level you consciously inhabit...
🔗
...and because they allow you to fight a more complex adversary (has more conscious levels than you) without understanding it consciously
🔗
...this means adversarial actions that operate through abstraction leaks are necessarily stochastic. Lone wolf attacks are like farts
🔗
"Safe spaces" are abstraction layers to those who want them, abstraction leaks to those who don't want them
🔗
My favorite fictional example of an abstraction leak (and the clearest/most literal) is the deja-vu scene in The Matrix.
🔗
If you're into Boydian stuff, the idea of "get inside adversary's decision cycle" is "engineer a leak in their operating abstraction level"
