“A research area dies when you have to spend more time figuring out whom you must…”
🔗
A research area dies when you have to spend more time figuring out whom you must cite to avoid pointless arguments than thinking about ideas
🔗
Research market supply/demand: People leave due to citation burden, field settles, a history is written, citation burden down, people return
🔗
During the over-supply phase, citations that are demanded for political/socialization reasons are basically an economic rent
🔗
This dynamic is why fields go through "ages" and "renaissances"... relative balance of citation load (tx costs) and work load creates cycles
